Our Legacy

Our Historic Journey

Founded in 1993, AIM POINT STUDY CIRCLE has nurtured generations of young minds into board toppers, professionals, and responsible nation-builders.

1993

The Inception

Established with a dedicated mission to bring high-standard, structured coaching for school students in Jamshedpur.

2000

Board Mastery & Expansion

Expanded to full-scale CBSE, ICSE, and JAC board programs with specialized faculty for Science and Mathematics.

2008

Senior Secondary Wing

Launched dedicated XI & XII Science batches (Physics, Chemistry, Maths, Biology) with foundational training for competitive entrance exams.

2020

Unbroken Learning During COVID-19

Even amid global lockdowns, education never stopped at Aim Point. We swiftly conducted interactive online classes, and our dedicated students attended with great interest and enthusiasm, ensuring zero loss of learning.

2024 & Beyond

Smart & Hybrid Learning

Serving over 5,000 alumni worldwide, continuously modernizing study materials and digital testing while preserving individual mentorship.

Our Methodology

The Aim Point Success Formula

A holistic 4-step pedagogical system designed to turn potential into performance.

1. Concept Clarity

We deconstruct complex scientific and mathematical concepts into intuitive, easy-to-grasp fundamentals before moving into advanced problem solving.

2. Daily Practice (DPP)

Daily and weekly problem sheets ensure regular practice, reinforcing lessons taught in class and building computational speed and accuracy.

3. One-on-One Doubt Sessions

Dedicated question-answering hours allow students to resolve every confusion without hesitation, ensuring zero backlog of difficult topics.
