Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common queries regarding admissions, classes, and teaching methodology.

Which classes and boards are taught at AIM POINT STUDY CIRCLE?
We provide comprehensive coaching for students from Class K.G. to Class XII across CBSE, ICSE, and JAC boards, as well as senior secondary science streams (Physics, Chemistry, Maths, Biology).
How can I register or take admission?
You can fill out our online Admission Form directly through the website, or visit our campus at Ashok Nagar, Near Vivek Vidyalaya, Chhota Govindpur, Jamshedpur.
Are separate doubt-clearing sessions conducted?
Yes! In addition to regular lecture sessions, we offer dedicated one-on-one doubt clearing periods and weekly assessment workshops to ensure no concept is left unresolved.
What are the batch timings?
We operate morning and evening batches to comfortably suit school schedules. Detailed batch timings based on your class and board will be provided upon admission inquiry.
